# Support

Mode: Operate. Members seek help on phones or desktop in ordinary indoor light;
both existing themes must remain legible. Inherit apps/web/DESIGN.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A private correspondence workspace with a clear next action and status.
The task determines hierarchy; no decorative dashboard metrics.

OWN-WORLD: Paper & Signal tokens, Geist/Estedad, navy ink, cool paper, restrained
white panels and existing rounded controls. Primary submissions use #0864D1.

STORY: Members understand who can see the conversation, choose a category and
send a useful message. Staff scan waiting tickets and respond without losing context.
Support staff means verified marketplace administrators and moderators; other
members see only their own tickets. Privacy copy names both staff roles.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Support heading and privacy explanation above a compact list;
New ticket is the single primary action. The form is one readable column. A
thread starts with its subject and status, followed by chronological messages.

FORM: Existing Operate workspace conventions; seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Acceptance: English/Persian RTL, both themes, 320/768/1280 widths, keyboard focus,
empty/loading/error/pending states, readable long messages and private screenshots.
