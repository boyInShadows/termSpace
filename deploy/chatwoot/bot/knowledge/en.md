# TermSpace knowledge (English)

## What TermSpace is
TermSpace (https://termspace.dev) is a free bilingual (English and Persian) community library and discovery platform for agentic coding tools. The catalog includes skills, agents, subagents, MCP servers, integrations, rules, prompts, hooks, templates, and workflows. TermSpace helps developers answer three questions: which tool solves my problem, will it work with my platform and workflow, and can I trust it enough to install or use it.

## Cost
Everything published on TermSpace is free. There is no checkout, no subscription, no paid tier for catalog resources, no payouts, and no creator revenue features. Listing or downloading a tool through TermSpace never charges the visitor.

## Accounts
Browsing is open to everyone without an account. A free account is required to favorite items, add them to a personal library, rate, or review. Visitors can create an account at /account with email and password, or continue with Google. Reviews require a verified email address; password reset and email verification are available from the sign-in flows. Signed-in readers can export their account data and delete their account from the account settings.

## Discovering tools
The catalog supports search plus filters for item type, community, platform compatibility, category, and rating. Platform and community are separate facets, so a listing can appear in several communities without duplicating its releases, reviews, or acquisition history. Each listing shows its declared permissions and access, its source, and pinned release information.

TermSpace is a catalog and discovery layer: it does not execute community tools, and it does not install them on the visitor's behalf. Before installing or using any tool, review its source and requested permissions — the listing links to the external source.

## Creators and publishing
Creators work from the dashboard (Studio). A creator can create owner-scoped drafts, manage releases and documentation, declare platform compatibility, and submit items for moderation. The first milestone references external GitHub or npm sources: TermSpace does not host or execute uploaded packages, and every published release resolves to an immutable source identity.

Publishing is free. There are no fees, no revenue share, and no paid promotion. Submissions go through staff moderation before or after publication, according to the moderation policy. Ownership transfer is a controlled, auditable process.

## Trust and moderation
Community submissions are treated as untrusted content, because agentic tools may contain executable code or instructions. TermSpace records release provenance and moderation audit events, validates submission metadata on the server, and provides reporting, takedown, suspension, and review-appeal workflows. Being listed is not an endorsement: TermSpace does not audit or guarantee every line of third-party code. Reviews are authenticated and rate-limited, and ratings are calculated from stored review records — creators cannot buy or edit ratings.

## Languages and themes
TermSpace is fully bilingual in English and Persian, with right-to-left layout and Persian typography, and it supports light and dark themes.

## Blog
The TermSpace Blog is a separate, staff-managed editorial publication about agentic coding. Marketplace accounts and creator roles do not grant blog publishing access.

## Privacy and data
Reader accounts are shared between the main site and the blog, but creator, moderator, and editorial permissions stay separate. Visitors can export their data and delete their account from the dashboard settings. Session cookies are httpOnly and host-only.

## Support and contact
For account problems, moderation questions, takedowns, privacy requests, or partnerships, email support@termspace.dev. Termbuddy, this chat, is an automated assistant available 24/7. It can be wrong about details; if the answer matters, confirm by email. There are no human agents answering live chat.

## What TermSpace is not
TermSpace is not an app store for paid software, not a code execution service or sandbox, not a guarantee that a listed third-party tool is safe, and not affiliated with the tools it lists. It does not sell user data.

## Common questions
- Do I need an account to browse? No. You need one to favorite, build a library, rate, or review.
- Are the tools paid? No. All published resources are free.
- Can I publish? Yes. Create a creator submission in the dashboard and submit it for moderation.
- How long does moderation take? There is no guaranteed timeline; moderation decisions and any follow-up are handled by staff.
- Can I get a refund? There is nothing to refund; TermSpace does not sell catalog resources.
- Is Persian supported? Yes, the whole site works in Persian with RTL layout, and Termbuddy answers in Persian.
- How do I report a tool? Use the report action on the listing; staff review reports and can take content down.
- Can I delete my account? Yes, from the dashboard account settings; data export is available there too.
